## Context

What problem, ambiguity, or force requires a durable constraint, stated in terms a
future contributor can verify from the current repository.

## Decision

The constraint, stated as invariants. Point to the owning specification, code, or
test for current-state detail rather than restating it.

## Consequences

What becomes required, forbidden, easier, or harder as a result, including what a
change to this decision would have to reconcile.

## Alternatives

Only the alternatives whose rejection still explains the constraint.
