## Independent reconstruction (performed before reading the report's reasoning)

### Decision at stake

Whether `Evidence`/`EvidenceUse` semantics are settled enough for a focused ADR/architecture reconciliation, after which — and only after which — a first headless implementation could be admitted.

### Constraints reconstructed from current authority

Repository facts, by status:

- **Accepted.** `ModelFingerprint` (content identity) and `ControlledRevisionId` (historical occurrence) are distinct; equal content recurs in distinct revisions (`R1 -> F1, R2 -> F2, R3 -> F1`); the revision core is immutable and corrections never rewrite an accepted record; evidence, evaluation, approval, and deployment are separate records that reference a revision (ADR-0004/0006/0008). `RevisionRecorder` is recording provenance, not attribution or authorization.
- **Accepted.** `EngineSemanticsVersion` is mandatory result-affecting Engine provenance; reproducibility inputs are fingerprint + semantics version + explicit workload + seed/random inputs + ordered commands; `RunId` is correlation only; durability is attribution plus a verifiable definition, not permanent re-execution; retirement removes executability, not provenance; **cross-version comparison is explicit and belongs to the consumer, naming Governance `EvidenceUse` as an example** (ADR-0015 decisions 2, 10, 14–17). This already settles who owns cross-version applicability — the brief's second falsification condition applies to any report that treated it as open.
- **Accepted.** Raw external observations never carry Arcogine identity at ingestion; they retain source identity, external subject identity, source/event time, receipt time, quality, trust/authenticity provenance, and raw-source reference; association is made later by a separate identity-bearing relating record; the original provenance is never rewritten, annotated, or duplicated; acceptance is monotonic and correction/retraction/supersession are new records about an earlier one (ADR-0013 §§5, 10, 11). ADR-0013 §11 also records a **known limit**: `EvidenceUse` binds evidence to a *point* identity, whereas an accountable operational continuation is *accumulating*, so the `Evidence`/`EvidenceUse` precedent transfers for *where* a binding lives, not for *what a binding to an accumulating identity means over time*.
- **Accepted.** Governance interchange formats are projections; no premature generic evidence ontology; serialization never defines identity (ADR-0012 §§1, 3, 12–14).
- **Durable rule.** Four kinds of provenance stay distinct (attributable actor, recording provenance, decision provenance, external data source); an external data source is not automatically an actor; `RequirementSource.authority` means publishing body, not authorization; there is no universal subject or actor reference (Overview, attribution boundaries).
- **Landed code.** `ConformanceEvaluation` carries requirement/assertion id+version, fingerprint, optional revision (verified through `ControlledRevisionAuthority`), result, and a `Finding` only for `FAIL`; it has no evaluation-occurrence identity, no temporal frame, and no evidence set. `EXTERNAL_EVIDENCE_REQUIRED` yields `UNKNOWN`; `NOT_APPLICABLE` arises only from scope. The evaluator never reads a clock. **`Requirement`/`Assertion` equality is id+version only; wording, source, and rule are excluded from equality; `RequirementCatalogue` is an immutable in-memory map with no persisted definition authority.** `GovernanceModuleBoundaryTest` actively forbids `Evidence`/`EvidenceUse` declarations. Product Java contains no `EngineSemanticsVersion` declaration; `RuntimeObservationMetadata` and `RuntimeEventEnvelope` carry `RunId` and `ModelFingerprint` (the envelope also an optional revision) but not the ADR-0015 semantics version, so Engine-side provenance propagation is incomplete.
- **Proposed / hypotheses.** Governance architecture §9's `Evidence`/`EvidenceUse` sketch (including “`Evidence` … does not carry a model fingerprint or revision” and the structural-evidence collapse); ADR-0016 decisions 1–10; Operational §8 observation contract and §8.1 correspondence.
- **Delivery state.** The Governance plan's evidence slice is dependency-blocked with explicit promotion criteria; the analytics-boundary research question is `READY`, so analytical-definition provenance ownership is unresolved.

### Candidate answers reconstructed

The brief's four (source record + contextual uses; context-bound identity; artifact/fact + interpretation layer; no new shared contract), plus three the reviewer derived independently as possible omissions:

- **R1 — Reference-only, no Governance-owned evidence record.** Governance owns only the use; evidence identity is a typed reference to a producer-owned identity (fingerprint/revision for structural facts, a run/result handle for Engine output, an Operational observation identity for external facts).
- **R2 — Content-addressed identity.** Identity is a digest of canonical content; reuse is content equality.
- **R3 — Versioned mutable evidence.** A stable evidence id with monotonic versions, mirroring `Requirement` id+version, with corrections as new versions.

## What was challenged, evidence used, and result

The report was then read in full at `23fe823` and each of its load-bearing conclusions was tested against the reconstruction above, the failure classes in §9, and the ten question-specific cases in the handoff.

### Load-bearing conclusions under test

1. Immutable source/result **reference** plus contextual **use**, with the roles distinguishable even when represented together.
2. Identity equality means the same referenced record revision — not byte equality, logical equivalence, common subject, corroboration, trust, or applicability; non-rebinding is mandatory.
3. Evidence may retain **producer-intrinsic** model/revision provenance; the blanket “no fingerprint on evidence” statement is too broad.
4. Evidence applicability, requirement applicability, and conformance outcome are three separate things; unusable evidence never yields `PASS` or `NOT_APPLICABLE` by itself.
5. IDs/version labels are insufficient for historical explanation without exact definition resolution, which current contracts do not supply.
6. Durable destination: reconcile through an expanded ADR-0016 plus Governance architecture §§7, 9, 12–13; implementation stays blocked.

### Challenges that succeeded (qualifications that must survive reconciliation)

None falsifies a load-bearing conclusion. Each narrows what a reconciliation may claim.

**Q-A. Evaluation-occurrence identity is a distinct identity the contract depends on and partially decides without its own candidate analysis** (reviewer case L). The recommended use contract binds evidence to “one identifiable evaluation occurrence”, fixes “an evaluation's actual basis”, and states “equal outcomes from two evaluations do not make them the same historical occurrence” — that last sentence is an equality rule for a new durable identity. On current `main`, `ConformanceEvaluation` is a pure-function value with no occurrence identity and no acceptance boundary; the report acknowledges “evaluation-history acceptance” as open but still relies on the occurrence as the anchor for use identity (“an evaluation-local use position plus an exact evaluation reference”). *Effect:* the evidence/use conclusion survives, but the reconciliation must **explicitly** either (i) decide evaluation-occurrence identity/immutability/acceptance with the same care as the evidence reference (distinct from `Finding`, from the evaluator's deterministic function, and from any audit projection), or (ii) name it as an unresolved prerequisite that the first implementation must satisfy before claiming historical attribution. It must not arrive as an unexamined consequence of the evidence decision.

**Q-B. Use-target semantics are established for point identities only** (reviewer case K). The report's use binds to “target model fingerprint, optional verified controlled revision, semantic subject/scope”. ADR-0013 §11 records that binding evidence to an *accumulating* accountable operational continuation is an open question. The report does not claim otherwise, but neither does it state the boundary. *Effect:* the reconciliation must state that the contract's applicability/target semantics cover fingerprint/revision/scope targets and that a use whose target is an operational continuation (or any other accumulating identity) is out of scope until ADR-0013's deferred question is answered.

**Q-C. Producer-identity availability bounds the first implementation's acceptance evidence.** Of the three provenance classes, only structural facts have a landed producer identity (fingerprint + authoritative revision). Engine-derived results lack a durable result identity (`RunId` is correlation-only; no persisted result record) and lack `EngineSemanticsVersion` propagation; external observations have no landed Operational observation type or correspondence authority; analytical-definition provenance ownership awaits the analytics-boundary question. The report is honest about this (“a fixture can test carrying an explicitly attributed analytical result, but cannot demonstrate a production adapter obtaining missing provenance”). *Effect:* a reconciliation or plan promotion must not present fixture-backed proof of the analytical or external classes as proof of integration; those classes remain blocked on their own prerequisites, and the ADR must record which class the first implementation actually proves.

**Q-D. The report's own three qualifications are corroborated, not merely accepted.** Qualification 3 (definition resolution) was reached independently by the reviewer from `Requirement`/`Assertion` equality and the in-memory catalogue before the report was read (case H); qualification 1 is consistent with ADR-0016 §6 and ADR-0015 §10; qualification 2 is consistent with the landed `NOT_APPLICABLE`-from-scope-only behavior. *Effect:* all three must be carried into the ADR text, not left in the report.

### Editorial notes (not qualifications)

- The SQL Server temporal-tables source is *system-versioned* only; Microsoft itself names the system-period columns `ValidFrom`/`ValidTo`, so the report's remark that database “valid” time need not be observed-fact time is fair against that source. In SQL:2011/bitemporal usage, however, “valid time” denotes application time by definition. Any reconciliation text should say “system/transaction time is not observed-fact time” rather than reuse the word “valid”, to avoid a terminology collision with standards vocabulary. No effect on the conclusion.
- The report's contract prose is dense; reconciliation should translate it into ADR decisions with one obligation per numbered item, as ADR-0015 does.

## Unresolved questions and missing evidence

Carried from the report and still open after review (none blocks the semantic split):

- exact reference/identifier representation, alias/deduplication mechanics, and capture format;
- consumer-specific freshness, admissibility, conflict-resolution, coverage, and compatibility policies;
- the Operational correspondence/trust capability and Operational observation identity required by any real external-observation integration;
- Engine result identity, `EngineSemanticsVersion` propagation, and analytical-definition provenance ownership (analytics-boundary question);
- exact requirement/assertion definition resolution and evaluation-history acceptance (now sharpened as Q-A);
- retention/compaction limits and truthful disclosure of missing history.

Newly surfaced by this review: whether evaluation-occurrence identity needs its own bounded research or can be decided within the ADR reconciliation with ordinary independent PR review. The reviewer's judgment is that the report's stated rules for it are sound and narrow enough to decide in the reconciliation, provided they are written as explicit decisions; a reconciliation reviewer who disagrees should route it to the research register rather than accept it implicitly.

## Final adversarial disposition

**ACCEPT WITH QUALIFICATIONS.**

The load-bearing conclusion — an immutable, source-qualified evidence reference plus contextual, attributable use, with non-rebinding, explicit applicability separate from requirement applicability and outcome, and exact definition resolution as a prerequisite to historical meaning — survives an independent attempt to falsify it. The qualifications that must survive any reconciliation are the report's own three plus Q-A (evaluation-occurrence identity decided or deferred explicitly), Q-B (use targets are point identities; accumulating operational continuations out of scope), and Q-C (first-implementation acceptance evidence bounded by the producer identities that actually exist).

Independence status: **independent — fresh isolated run** (operating-model second tier); different model family not verifiable. This satisfies the high-risk prerequisite as the operating model defines it; it does not itself accept ADR-0016, change current architecture, or promote the Governance plan. Durable promotion remains a separate reconciliation change with its own independent PR review.
