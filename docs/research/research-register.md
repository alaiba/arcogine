# Research register

> **Status:** Maintained research portfolio/lifecycle state  
> **Scope:** Admitted Arcogine research questions and their current priority, lifecycle state, evidence artifact, expected destination, and review date  
> **Authority:** Research portfolio state only; not accepted product/architecture semantics or implementation commitment

The normative research operating model — including what qualifies as research, lifecycle meanings, priority semantics, promotion/reconciliation, evidence custody, synthesis-seed handling, and register maintenance/compaction — is defined in [`docs/development/researching.md`](../development/researching.md).

Priority is portfolio guidance, not delivery commitment.
