## Seeds

### Durable identity contracts drift into their own storage representation

- **Signal** — When a durable identity or provenance contract states its invariant in terms of what is *kept* (records retained, fields carried, a log prefix preserved), it silently fixes a storage representation and then mis-classifies legitimate representation changes as semantic changes. The repair in both observed instances was the same move: restate the invariant as what must remain **answerable or provable**, and leave *where the supporting material lives* to implementation.
- **Origin** — Operational durable-identity investigation and its independent adversarial review. Source report: `bf744af171d5a42a3c578d33836e8e45bd33b0fe` + `docs/research/operational-execution-digital-twin-identity-report.md`. Adversarial review (`ACCEPT WITH QUALIFICATIONS`): `f6a3b7148fadc9c29652f5bf269606333299496c` + `docs/research/operational-identity-adversarial-review.md`. Successor correction: `794b65b70c1849e13eae592d0bce053006382050` + `docs/research/investigations/operational-identity-adversarial-review-corrections.md`. Durable destination: [ADR-0013: Durable operational identity](../architecture/decisions/0013-durable-operational-identity.md) §4 and §7.
- **Evidence posture** — Two independent instances inside one investigation, each caught by adversarial challenge rather than by the original author. A continuity condition written as "nothing accepted is dropped or forgotten" would have made compaction, archival, and storage migration into identity forks, contradicting a requirement the same document asserted. A divergence-detectability obligation written as "every accepted record must carry its carried-forward evidence" promoted one external system's per-record layout into the semantic contract. In both cases the underlying discriminator was sound and only the framing was wrong, so the defect was invisible to a reader who agreed with the conclusion. This is an Arcogine observation about how these two contracts were drafted and corrected; it is not established as a general property of identity modelling.
- **Boundaries / counterevidence** — One investigation only, in one problem domain, with a single reviewer; no independent cross-investigation recurrence yet. The inverse error is real and not addressed here: an invariant stated so abstractly that no implementation can be checked against it. The representation-neutral framing also costs something concrete — a per-record layout is mechanically verifiable, whereas "must remain provable" defers verification to whoever designs the record capability.
- **Reusable assets** — The two corrected formulations themselves, retained in ADR-0013 §4 and §7. The discriminating proving cases that expose the defect: declared-and-bounded compaction versus silent loss; an *undeclared* divergence, which ancestry links written only at declared forks cannot detect. Both are cheap to re-run against any future identity, provenance, or evidence contract.
- **Occurrences** — None recorded yet beyond the originating investigation. The Agency and decision-boundary investigation is a plausible neighbour — the source report notes it shares the shape of durable attribution surviving mechanism change — but its reconciliation is outstanding, so nothing is recorded as an occurrence here.
- **Revisit when** — A second, independently conducted Arcogine investigation produces an identity, provenance, retention, or evidence contract whose first formulation has to be corrected the same way; or the first durable operational record capability finds ADR-0013 §7 either unimplementable or trivially satisfiable, which would be direct evidence for or against the framing.
