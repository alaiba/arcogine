## Seeds

### Durable identity contracts drift into their own storage representation

- **Signal** — When a durable identity or provenance contract states its invariant in terms of what is *kept* (records retained, fields carried, a log prefix preserved), it silently fixes a storage representation and then mis-classifies legitimate representation changes as semantic changes. The repair in both observed instances was the same move: restate the invariant as what must remain **answerable or provable**, and leave *where the supporting material lives* to implementation.
- **Origin** — Operational durable-identity investigation, its independent adversarial review (`ACCEPT WITH QUALIFICATIONS`), and a successor correction note that narrowed two of the review's claims. Evidence reference: reconciliation PR #314, which records the exact report, review, and correction coordinates. Durable destination: [ADR-0013: Durable operational identity](../architecture/decisions/0013-durable-operational-identity.md) §4 and §7.
- **Evidence posture** — Two independent instances inside one investigation, each caught by adversarial challenge rather than by the original author. A continuity condition written as "nothing accepted is dropped or forgotten" would have made compaction, archival, and storage migration into identity forks, contradicting a requirement the same document asserted. A divergence-detectability obligation written as "every accepted record must carry its carried-forward evidence" promoted one external system's per-record layout into the semantic contract. In both cases the underlying discriminator was sound and only the framing was wrong, so the defect was invisible to a reader who agreed with the conclusion. This is an Arcogine observation about how these two contracts were drafted and corrected; it is not established as a general property of identity modelling.
- **Boundaries / counterevidence** — Two investigations now, but in adjacent problem domains and with the same reviewing discipline rather than genuinely varied method; the second occurrence below is also a weaker instance than the originating two. No instance has yet been observed outside durable identity/provenance contract drafting. The inverse error is real and not addressed here: an invariant stated so abstractly that no implementation can be checked against it. The representation-neutral framing also costs something concrete — a per-record layout is mechanically verifiable, whereas "must remain provable" defers verification to whoever designs the record capability.
- **Reusable assets** — The two corrected formulations themselves, retained in ADR-0013 §4 and §7. The discriminating proving cases that expose the defect: declared-and-bounded compaction versus silent loss; an *undeclared* divergence, which ancestry links written only at declared forks cannot detect. Both are cheap to re-run against any future identity, provenance, or evidence contract.
- **Occurrences** — **Independent occurrence, 2026-09-14, Agency and decision-boundary investigation.** Its independent adversarial review stated `RevisionRecorder`'s semantic contract in terms of the record's stored field decomposition — `source` as the recording mechanism, `subject` as the actor-like party — and presented that split as repository fact. The successor correction moved the contract back to the level at which durable authority actually speaks: the recorder is recording provenance **as a whole**, and its internal `source` / `subject` decomposition is underspecified by ADR-0008 and by the implementation. Evidence reference: reconciliation PR #316, which records the exact review and correction coordinates. Durable destination: [Architecture Overview — Attribution and decision boundaries](../architecture/overview.md#attribution-and-decision-boundaries) and [Governance and Conformance Architecture](../architecture/governance-conformance.md) §4. Recorded as **independent**, not reuse: the correction predates this seed, so the seed was not a premise or framing input to it. **Materially similar aspect** — a semantic contract and the representation carrying it were conflated, and the repair in every instance separated the two. **Where it differs, stated so the similarity is not overread** — the originating instances mis-stated a contract Arcogine was *drafting*, while this one mis-read a contract Arcogine had *already persisted*, and the failure direction is inverted: representation fixed by the contract there, semantics inferred from representation here. The same review drew the same inference on a second field, reading `ChangeProvenance.source` as a role label; that one was caught at reconciliation review rather than by the successor correction. Two fields in one review, corrected at two different stages, is recorded as one occurrence rather than two.
- **Revisit when** — A second, independently conducted Arcogine investigation produces an identity, provenance, retention, or evidence contract whose first formulation has to be corrected the same way; or the first durable operational record capability finds ADR-0013 §7 either unimplementable or trivially satisfiable, which would be direct evidence for or against the framing. **Surfaced for an explicit decision:** the first condition is now arguably met by the Agency occurrence above, in the weaker form noted there. This record creates no research question, priority, or work by itself; whether a bounded cross-investigation synthesis question should be admitted to the [research register](research-register.md) is a deliberate decision that has not been taken.
