## Research result to reconcile

Do **not** reconcile the original report's broad wording. Reconcile the result as narrowed by the
independent adversarial review.

The surviving proposition is:

> Under current deterministic, non-spatial Factory/Engine semantics, a deliberately small serial
> fixed-workload challenge can exhibit mechanically non-nested capital/capacity trade-offs. A bounded
> reference family using shared multi-step-eligible capacity provides a robust constructive case:
> at least two materially separated, nondominated structures survive all tested resource-identity
> orderings and bounded economic perturbation. Shared eligibility is not established as necessary;
> the tested dedicated-only family usually collapses toward nested capacity purchase but contains
> smaller finite-batch exceptions.

The strongest reference separation is the S1/S2 pair:

- S1 — cutter + assembler + two inspectors; cost 850; completion 56
- S2 — cutter + assembler + inspector + shared assembly/inspection resource; cost 913; completion
  46–47 over all tested resource-type identity orders

The review found this pair remains separated even at a uniform 15% completion-improvement threshold.
S3 remains mechanically distinct and nondominated in the selected reference, but its incremental
completion separation from S2 is only 1–2 ticks. Do **not** reconcile a claim that all three designs are
pairwise materially separated.

## Qualifications that must survive reconciliation

The adversarial review's qualifications are part of the accepted result, not optional commentary.

### Shared eligibility is constructive, not necessary

Do not preserve or restate the original claim that useful strategy space exists **only** with
multi-step-eligible resources.

The reviewed result is:

- shared eligibility produced the strongest and most robust constructive family tested;
- dedicated-only cases usually behaved like nested capacity purchase in the tested family;
- finite-batch dedicated exceptions exist and passed the original proving criteria;
- therefore exclusion of shared offers is **not** automatically a negative/falsification result.

Any durable product requirement may select a shared-capacity reference challenge because it is a
strong proving case, but it must not turn that selection into a universal semantic necessity.

### Occupancy is measurement, not a causal bottleneck definition

The adversarial review falsified the generalized equation:

`maximum connected-pool occupancy == active causal constraint`.

A constructed case had CUT as the highest pool-occupancy measurement while adding cutter capacity had
no completion effect and adding inspection/shared capacity did.

Durable reconciliation may preserve:

- occupancy as descriptive supported/research-local measurement;
- controlled marginal capacity interventions as evidence for the selected reference's effective
  constraint migration.

Do not define a repository-wide bottleneck/constraint algorithm here, and do not settle analytics
ownership. The READY analytics-ownership and diagnostic-evidence investigations keep their authority.

For the selected reference family, the independently exercised intervention sequence supports:

1. inspection capacity matters first;
2. after relieving inspection, assembly capacity matters;
3. after relieving assembly, cutting capacity matters.

Phrase this as a bounded proving result, not a universal scheduling theorem.

### Resource identity/order remains result-affecting current semantics

The adversarial review strengthened the order-sensitivity analysis:

- the report's C/R block orders are **not** guaranteed performance extremes;
- some affordable shared-resource designs have worse intermediate type interleavings;
- the selected S1/S2/S3 structures nevertheless remain nondominated under the exhaustive tested
  resource-type order sweep;
- S2 completes in 46–47 ticks and its shared resource serves both assembly and inspection in every
  tested ordering.

Reconcile only the supported integration consequence:

> A future game/consumer projector operating under current Engine semantics must make
> result-affecting resource identity/order explicit and deterministic.

Do **not** assign that policy to Challenge as established ownership. Do not promote the separate
shared-resource final-tie-break CANDIDATE or reopen `combinedQueueDepth` merely from this result.
Where identity allocation lives, how edits preserve it, and whether current `MachineId` tie-breaking
is an acceptable player-visible policy remain separate questions.

### Materiality is definition-dependent

Do not promote the report's large prevalence counts as an objective measurement of "meaningful
strategy space".

The original classifier treated pooling and dedicated mechanisms differently. The adversarial review
showed that uniform completion-separation thresholds materially change prevalence, while the selected
S1/S2 existence result remains strong.

Prefer the concrete fixed-reference proving case over aggregate "735/271" style prevalence claims in
durable product requirements.

Do not invent a universal materiality percentage. State the selected reference's demonstrated
separation and its bounded robustness.

### Economics are bounded challenge content, not a law

The reference's fixed budget/deadline survives a useful range of flex pricing; the positive is not a
one-credit knife edge. But no universal `1.0–1.5x` equipment-price rule was established.

Reconcile:

- one concrete reference economic basis, or a deliberately bounded allowed range if product planning
  genuinely needs it;
- sufficient budget slack to permit real alternatives;
- the principle that challenge economics are game-owned.

Do not promote a universal pricing ratio.

### Mechanical strategy is not human comprehension

The investigation establishes **mechanical ground truth** only.

It can establish:

- non-identical structures;
- nondominance under declared dimensions;
- material performance differences in the selected pair;
- actual shared-resource usage;
- controlled intervention effects;
- bounded capital/performance trade-offs.

It does not establish:

- engagement;
- player comprehension;
- discoverability;
- fairness of presentation;
- learning efficacy;
- population-level interpretation.

Keep those with diagnostic-evidence, equipment-value-discovery, scoring/level and external-player
research as applicable.

Do not require every catalogue offer to be nondominated. Dominated or mechanically equivalent offers
may still be useful product content for bargain/opportunity discovery, presentation or player
preference. Conversely, perceived value cannot rescue a mechanically trivial kernel.

### Current-semantics scope

Keep the conclusion explicitly bounded to:

- current deterministic Engine interpretation;
- non-spatial execution;
- always-online resources in the proving family;
- one fixed quantity-bearing production requirement;
- serial routing with unequal step durations;
- current step-level duration semantics;
- current explicit eligibility/resource-capacity semantics.

Do not infer conclusions for:

- branching or longer routings;
- multiple products/orders;
- spatial transfer;
- resource-dependent speed/performance;
- executable setup/changeover;
- generalized resource characteristics/qualification;
- machine failures/repairs;
- stochastic arrivals/yield/duration.

Those are separate existing/open research questions or future reopening conditions.

### Twin/singles control stays narrow

The tested twin-versus-two-singles result supports only that equal total concurrency produced equal
aggregate completion in the tested always-online workload family.

Do not turn it into:

- Factory content equivalence;
- equality of dispatch assignments;
- a ban on differently priced/presented catalogue offers;
- a universal statement under availability changes or richer resource semantics.
