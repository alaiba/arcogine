## Independently reconstructed decision and constraints

The decision is whether current production semantics admit a bounded mechanical challenge
worth taking into separate product reconciliation. It is not whether a particular game is fun.
Risk is medium for bounded product evidence, with high-risk identity/determinism and ownership
edges if generalized into Engine or projector requirements. Apply independent scrutiny to those
edges without turning this review into a dispatch redesign.

- One fixed quantity-bearing order; Engine owns decomposition. One serial routing and no spatial
  record. Current Engine refuses spatial-present execution.
- Eligibility is explicit; duration belongs to each step. Concurrency is executable capacity.
  Setup and volumetric fields are observable authored facts without current execution effects.
- Shared eligibility is supported. Immediate acceptance, combined waiting ranking, final
  MachineId order, local FIFO and shared-backlog reselection can all matter.
- MachineIds and resource-list ordering are canonical content. Permuting IDs is therefore an
  input sensitivity experiment, not a failure of same-input determinism. A player could still
  regard identity allocation as arbitrary.
- Catalogue prices/budgets are consumer economics. Challenge is independent of Factory; a future
  consumer projector is not yet implemented. Existing research oracles are research-local.
- Occupancy over the run is descriptive. Causal marginal capacity value requires controlled
  interventions and can depend on the finite horizon, starvation, and dispatch interactions.
- A complete frontier depends on explicit objectives, feasibility, bounds and equivalence rules.
  Mechanical dominance does not decide the offered human choice set or preference.

## Candidate answers

1. Null: dedicated or pooled designs all collapse into a nested capacity ladder or distinctions
   too fragile to survive modest parameter/order changes.
2. Dedicated-only alternative: finite-batch pipeline balancing can produce crossing allocations
   under capital constraints even without flexibility. Search before making a universal negative.
3. Pooling alternative: reallocatable concurrency across steps creates genuine substitution
   trade-offs, with scope conditional on durations, quantity, economics and current dispatch.
4. Identity-policy explanation: apparent pooling benefit is mainly favorable deterministic
   tie-breaking; a positive existence result might survive but fairness/robustness does not.
5. Economic-artifact explanation: catalogue discounts, duplicate execution-equivalent machines,
   budget/target selection or materiality arithmetic manufacture an apparent frontier.

## Discriminating attacks

- Recompute frontier and qualifying counts independently from persisted raw data, without the
  original classifier; check strict versus weak dominance and target inclusivity.
- Enumerate resource-type interleavings beyond two block orders. Hold all non-identity facts
  fixed, test target crossings and whether non-nested alternatives survive.
- Compare actual completion deltas for capacity additions at every operation, then repeat after
  the first improvement. Do not select a winner merely by maximum occupancy.
- Test alternative materiality cutoffs, nearby prices and targets, and a low-quantity or balanced
  routing negative control. Distinguish existential evidence from a universal pricing rule.
- Verify one workload, supported evidence, online availability, no hidden state, and fresh-run
  event/observation replay. Preserve original protocols/outputs.
- Check chronology of protocol revisions and make post-observation selection explicit.
- Bound claims about machine granularity, industrial realism, human comprehension and ownership.
